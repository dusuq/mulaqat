# Service catalog

Match each prospect to ONE primary service. Open with their likely problem, not the technology.
This is the default catalog (a software/automation agency). Replace or extend it in `mulaqat.config.md`.

| Service                         | Best-fit buyers                                        | Problem to open with                                            | Qualifying questions                                                    |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| WhatsApp AI agents & automation | E-commerce, clinics, real estate, schools, restaurants | Slow replies, lost leads, manual follow-up                      | Monthly WhatsApp volume? Who replies now? On the WhatsApp Business API? |
| CRM building                    | SMEs with sales teams, agencies, B2B services          | Leads scattered across spreadsheets and chats, no pipeline view | Leads per month? Where are they tracked today? Team size?               |
| POS systems                     | Retail, restaurants, pharmacies, multi-branch shops    | No live stock or sales view, branches not connected             | How many branches? Current POS? Need offline mode?                      |
| ERP software                    | Manufacturing, distribution, agriculture               | Disconnected systems, slow month-end reporting                  | Which departments? Current tools? Biggest reporting pain?               |
| Progressive Web Apps            | Startups, local service businesses                     | Native app too costly, weak mobile site                         | Who are the users? Need offline or push notifications?                  |
| WordPress development           | Small businesses, consultants, agencies (white-label)  | Slow or dated site that brings no leads                         | Current site? Goal: leads, bookings or sales?                           |
| E-commerce stores               | D2C brands, retailers moving online                    | No online store, or low conversion                              | Platform today? Monthly orders? Payment gateways needed?                |
| AI agents & workflow automation | Ops-heavy SMEs, support teams                          | Repetitive manual work, slow response times                     | Which task eats the most hours? Tools in use?                           |
| Meta & Google Ads               | Local businesses, e-commerce                           | Spending on ads with no clear return                            | Monthly ad budget? What's tracked today?                                |

If a prospect fits more than one, pick the one with the clearest pain and raise the other only after they engage.

## Writing a service card

For each service in the config, keep:

- **One-line outcome** — what changes for the buyer ("replies in under a minute, 24/7")
- **Proof** — 1–2 real results with numbers, or `[ADD REAL RESULT]`
- **Price band and timeline** — only if the user wants them mentioned in conversation
- **Qualifying questions** — 3, short
